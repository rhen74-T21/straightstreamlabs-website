Fireground Scenario Trainer

Tactical decision-making and incident command judgment

Our first release—a dedicated tactical decision simulator designed for company officers, incident commanders, and advancing firefighters. Puts you at the tip of the spear through high-stakes dispatch situations requiring rapid prioritization, crew safety assessment, and resource coordination.

  • 127 Validated Scenarios: Structure Fire, Vehicle Extrication, Fire Danger, and Incident Command System (ICS).
  • Milestone Achievements: Persistent, shareable training cards earned at 25, 50, and 100 decisions and 10-answer streaks.
  • 100% Offline & Private: No user accounts, no cloud sync, and zero behavioral telemetry. Progress stays entirely on your phone.

NREMT Exam Prep & Trainer

NREMT cognitive exam preparation & clinical judgment trainer

Our next release—built to elevate emergency medical practice beyond basic rote memorization. Connects didactic national curriculum to the pressure of live patient encounters with clinical discrimination, parallel distractors, and allowlisted medical rationale.

  • Core NREMT Domains: Airway & Respiration, Cardiology & Resuscitation, Medical Emergencies, Trauma, and EMS Operations.
  • Evidence-Grounded Rationales: Every intervention is traced to authoritative EMS standards and vetted clinical source locators.
  • Distractor Discrimination: Teaches clinicians why near misses fail, reinforcing proper assessment sequence and field safety.
