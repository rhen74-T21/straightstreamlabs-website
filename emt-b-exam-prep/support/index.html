Support

Questions, problems, or feedback?

Contact the publisher, Straight Stream Labs, LLC, for help with EMT-B Exam Prep.

support@straightstreamlabs.com

What to include in a bug report

  • Your phone or tablet model
  • Your operating system and version
  • The installed app version (check Android Settings → Apps → EMT-B Exam Prep; labels vary by device)
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and any error message
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Report a question or flashcard

Include the item ID if available, or enough of the wording to identify it, and explain the concern. You can use the same support email for content feedback.

Your progress and privacy

Support email does not automatically send us your study progress. Your message includes only the information you choose to send. See our privacy policy for information about local storage, Android backups, and support correspondence.

EMT-B Exam Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians (NREMT). It is not a substitute for an approved EMT education program and does not guarantee certification or predict exam results.
